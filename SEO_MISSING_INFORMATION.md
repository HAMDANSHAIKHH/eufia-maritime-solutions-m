# Missing Information Requiring Confirmation

The following points are not currently proven in the repository and should be confirmed before expanding trust signals or authority-building content:

- The exact legal or registered trading name used by the business beyond the names visible in the codebase.
- Official social media profile URLs.
- Verified office address details beyond those currently referenced in the app.
- Any actual service coverage claims beyond the scope already described in the existing project copy.
- Any investment, partnership or association references that are not already included in the website or repository.
- Any specific certification, award, crew or fleet claims that would need formal verification.
- Any local office or UAE/Dubai service claims that require official confirmation.

## Recommended approach

Keep all content factual and avoid claiming business facts that the repository or supplied project brief does not support. Any new business detail should be confirmed by the company before using it in SEO, schema.org markup, or public marketing copy.
