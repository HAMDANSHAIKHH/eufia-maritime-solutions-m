# SEO Content Strategy and Authority Plan

## Editorial objective

The goals are to support search relevance, AI understandability, and business-qualified enquiry generation without creating low-value pages or generic filler content.

## Recommended content pillars

1. Commercial services and capability pages
2. Maritime education and answer-engine content
3. Operational and compliance guidance for shipping stakeholders
4. International trade and logistics context for regional buyers
5. Structured conversion-oriented enquiry journeys

## Suggested content format

Each premium article should include:

- a useful problem-led heading,
- a clear audience and intent statement,
- practical explanation for the operational issue,
- relevant service links to the actual company offering,
- a CTA to contact EUFIA when appropriate,
- a publish date and review date.

## Editorial governance

- Do not publish low-value AI-generated pages for the sake of volume.
- Make sure each article distinguishes between general market knowledge and the actual EUFIA service offering.
- Keep facts anchored to operational realities.
- Add or review author attribution only if it is accurate and approved.
- Link each article back to the relevant commercial service page.

## CTA recommendations

Use CTA text that matches the article purpose, such as:

- “Discuss your vessel chartering requirements”
- “Request a dry bulk shipping proposal”
- “Plan your project cargo movement”
- “Ask about inspection and compliance support”

## Recommended next step

Create a small editorial pilot of a minimum viable set of 4-6 high-value articles rather than publishing a large volume of shallow content. Focus on the topics with the clearest commercial intent and strongest cross-links to service pages.
